## Instantly placement test (paid, Growth Inbox Placement $47/mo; now cancelled, 402, `PLACEMENT_TESTS_ENABLED=false`)
- `POST /internal/audit/placement-test/run` (type-1 one-time, `run_immediately:true`) seeds from the lifecycle TESTABLE pool `fetchTestablePoolEmails` = `lifecycle_status IN ('in_recovery','in_production')` AND >= `TESTABLE_MIN_AGE_DAYS` (7) days old (no `timestamp_created` counts as old enough; guard `testable-pool.test.ts`), NOT the send pool (bootstrap deadlock: an `in_recovery` account must be testable to earn promotion). **Weekly FULL pool on Sunday** (the only send-free day since Saturday became a sending day 2026-10-10): a test sends ~30-50 seeds/account and a mailbox's safe day is ~50, so it cannot ride a sending day. NO per-account rotation or cursor (`selectDailyTestBatch`/`fetchLastPlacementTestByAccount` removed; do not reintroduce); lever when the quota is short is a wider interval or smaller pool. Empty pool = `created:0`, no call. Gated by `PLACEMENT_TESTS_ENABLED=true` (409 off). Recurrence is OUR cron, not Instantly's scheduler. `POST .../placement-test/ensure` = type-2 automated, HyperGrowth-only (402), unused. `POST .../placement-test/sync` read-only (no quota), 202, `placement-sync: done`, idempotent.
- **Create-body (found by LIVE probe; the docs 404)**: required `type`, `sending_method:1`, `delivery_mode:1`, `email_subject`, `email_body`, `emails` (explicit SENDER emails; empty 400s `Either tags or emails must be provided`), `recipients_labels` (from `/inbox-placement-tests/email-service-provider-options`), `text_only`. Type-2 needs `schedule` = `{days: {"0".."6": bool} (OBJECT), timing:{from,to}, timezone: fixed enum (America/Chicago ok, Etc/UTC rejected)}`. Rediscover by one throwaway probe with a browser-ish `User-Agent`, not N hotfixes. Flat subscription: no cost declared.
- **`placement-cron.yml`: `sync` is DECOUPLED from `run` and fires DAILY (`0 7 * * *`); weekly `0 6 * * 0` does `accounts-sync` + `run`.** A test takes HOURS; chained `sync` polled a 4-minute-old test and never ingested it (results 7 days stale, a mid-week mailbox waited ~2 weeks). Weekly steps gated `if: github.event.schedule != '0 7 * * *'` (true for the weekly cron AND `workflow_dispatch`). Scheduled runs fire from `main` only. **`placement-prime-cron.yml` (`30 5 * * 0`) runs 30 min BEFORE the test**: `reactivate-accounts`, wait, then `accounts-sync` (the sync carries the resumed status into silver and runs `reconcileLifecycle`, moving the mailbox INTO the testable pool); a SEPARATE schedule (both endpoints 202; chaining races the pool read). Over-seeding is the cheap direction.
- **No second placement path**: `run-untested`, `runUntestedPlacementTest`, `fetchUntestedTestableEmails`, `UNTESTED_RETEST_SUPPRESSION_HOURS` and its hourly step were DELETED 2026-07-29; to cover a new account sooner move the weekly cron.

## In-house seed placement ($0/mo; `SEED_PLACEMENT_ENABLED=true` arms `/run`, default OFF/409, `/sync` ungated)
Supplies the receiver inboxes from mailboxes we own; dispatch is `self-send/smtp.ts`, folder read `self-send/imap-poller.ts`, aggregation `placement-promote.ts`.
- **Writes into the EXISTING silver**: `instantly_placement_results` (`test_id` is plain text) under a `seed:<uuid>` id (like `self:`/`reserving:`), so the lifecycle gate, account-health and history read it with zero code change. Do NOT add a parallel silver or second gate.
- Two bronze tables (migration `0044`): `seed_placement_dispatches` (DENOMINATOR) and `seed_placement_observations` (NUMERATOR); **`missing` = dispatched minus observed** (deriving the denominator from observations scores a black hole as `0/0` = untested). A FAILED dispatch is recorded but does not count (only `outcome='sent'`). **First observation wins** (unique `(receiver_email, message_id)`; delivery-time verdict). Poller idempotent without a cursor.
- **Only a mailbox on a consumer-relevant ESP can be a receiver; the ESP is read off the resolved IMAP HOST, never the domain** (`espForReceiverHost` returns null for Gandi and the Mailforge relay; `selectSeedReceivers` skips; never assume Google: the first version mislabelled six Gandi receivers as Gmail). Gandi/relay mailboxes are still good SENDERS. Zero gradeable receivers = log and stop.
- **Aliases: `authUser` is the login when it differs (`loginFor`)** at all three auth sites (`smtp.ts`, `imap-poller.ts`, seed `sync.ts`); 175 Instantly IMAP accounts sit on 44 Gandi mailboxes.
- Receivers: at most one per domain, `MAX_SEED_RECEIVERS` 10, deterministic (comparable week over week). A mailbox never seeds itself (`planSeedSends`). **The seed is deliberately ORDINARY** (does NOT use `buildEmailBodyWithSignature`: plain, link-free, no pixel; correlate on RFC 5322 `Message-Id`). Only folders carrying a verdict are read (`classifySeedFolder`: INBOX + spam; null for everything else, incl. `[Gmail]/All Mail`, which mirrors both and double-counts). Auth flags: absent mechanism = `null`, never `false`.
- **Credentials**: `seed-placement/credentials.ts` = manual `mailbox-credentials` platform key (JSON array `address, appPassword, smtpHost, imapHost`) before Primeforge; only a key-service 404 = none configured, 401/5xx/malformed throws. **`loadSeedCredentialResolver` loads every source ONCE and the pool is NARROWED to what we can authenticate BEFORE planning** (per-seed `resolveMailboxCredential` would paginate the vendor thousands of times); unresolvable mailbox = skipped (`skippedNoCredential`); receivers drawn from the sendable set (~25 senders x 10 receivers ~ 250 seeds). **A refusing vendor drops only ITS mailboxes here too** (`loadPrimeforgeMailboxes(caller, "skip")`, GANDI-OPEN-1030): the unguarded read would have killed every test from Primeforge's 402 of 2026-10-29. **All 10 Google receivers are Primeforge mailboxes and every manual credential is Gandi**: while Primeforge refuses us no test can grade at Google (Microsoft receivers still read).
- **Cadence `seed-placement-cron.yml`: BOTH steps DAILY, the run step decides itself (`decideSeedTestDue`, `seed-placement/due.ts`). The cadence lives in the DATA, not the scheduler (do NOT go back to a weekly cron; Actions skipped 2026-08-29's Saturday job; two misses age the fleet past the 16-day limit).** Rules in order: never tested = always due; evidence >= `SEED_EVIDENCE_URGENT_AGE_DAYS` (12) = due ANY day; inside `SEED_TEST_INTERVAL_DAYS` (6, not 7) = not due; interval elapsed on a sending day = wait for Sunday (the only non-sending day since 2026-10-10; a Saturday run would stack seeds on a full cold day, over the ~50/day mailbox cap); else due. `force:true` bypasses (manual only). **The "last test" is the last one that SENT a seed to a gate-grading receiver** (`fetchLastSeedTestAt`: `outcome='sent'`, ESP not in `MICROSOFT_ESPS`): a Microsoft-only test measures nothing the 16-day limit is about. **Health check `seed-placement-test`** (`health.ts`, hourly worker `seed-placement-health-worker.ts`, 60 s after boot) reports to features-service's watchdog registry (`POST /internal/box-checks/runs`, red on dashboard v2 Monitoring > Box > Watchdogs, never an email): red when that test is older than `SEED_TEST_OVERDUE_AGE_DAYS` (8) or no Google receiver is readable (names the refusing vendor); the registry reds it alone after 3 h without a report. Guard `seed-placement-health.test.ts`. **Do NOT chain the read onto the send** (daily 09:00 own tick). Reconcile runs after a sync only when `silverRows > 0`.
- **Fidelity caveat**: receivers are Google WORKSPACE mailboxes (more permissive than consumer `gmail.com`), so a mailbox can score well and still land in a real prospect's spam. The paid test was kept in parallel until scores agreed (shared-IP accounts must still score badly); if the harness scores everything ~100% it is not a gate: fix = consumer seed inboxes (a few `gmail.com`/`outlook.com`/`yahoo.com` app-password mailboxes via the receiver list), NOT lowering `PRODUCTION_DELIVERY_PCT_BAR`. Compare by `test_id` prefix; the gate takes the newest per account (`DISTINCT ON (account_email) ORDER BY tested_at DESC`).
- **Scope: only credentialed mailboxes are measured, and that set is EXPANDABLE** (Gandi API can SET a mailbox password; Mailforge exports credentials as a CSV from its UI; Instantly holds but does not return them). **Validate with a NEGATIVE CONTROL: load ONE known-Gmail-spam legacy mailbox into the manual key and run once** (bad score = harness discriminates; ~100% = Workspace receivers too permissive). An unmeasured account ages into `delivery_evidence_stale` (correct for the legacy fleet; `klourd@pressbeat.ai` needed its credential).

## Microsoft receivers and routing (MS-SEED-PLACEMENT-1018)
- **Microsoft seed receivers** (Outlook.com, Microsoft 365) are OUR mailboxes read through **Microsoft Graph**, never password IMAP (Microsoft refuses it). Authorized ONCE by a person with the OAuth device code flow (`microsoft-oauth.ts`): `POST /internal/seed-placement/microsoft/authorize {email}` answers `userCode` + `verificationUri`; the service polls in the background (resumed at boot), REFUSES a sign-in by any other account (`/me` must equal the email), stores the refresh token in `microsoft_seed_mailboxes` (migration `0085`), rotates it on every use, refreshes every authorized mailbox after each sync (keepalive). `GET /internal/seed-placement/microsoft` = state, never a token. Client id = Microsoft's public "Graph Command Line Tools" app (`MS_SEED_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID` overrides), tenant `common`, scope `Mail.Read User.Read offline_access`. $0.
- They only RECEIVE: appended to the Google receiver set beside `MAX_SEED_RECEIVERS`, `recipient_esp = 2`. Read = Graph `inbox` (Focused + Other = inbox, `ms_inference` kept) + `junkemail` (= spam), SCL kept (`ms_scl`); same bronze, same first-observation-wins, same silver.
- **Microsoft is a ROUTING signal, never a demotion**: the lifecycle gate (`fetchLatestDeliveryByAccount`) and the pooled `inboxPlacement` read every ESP EXCEPT `MICROSOFT_ESPS` (2, 4). **Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 are two filters, two codes (SENDER-HEALTH-1019)**: a receiver grades under `espForMicrosoftReceiver(account_kind)`: business = 2, consumer = 4 (unknown kind = not a receiver). Prod 2026-10-19: Microsoft 365 46/46 Inbox (SCL 1), Outlook.com 26/186 (SCL 6), 19 of 46 senders junked at 11:04 were Inbox at 11:22 with the same body (zero the other way) = the fresh consumer receiver, not the senders; consumer prospects = 4 of 24,118 sends in 30 d, Microsoft 365 = 2,782. Migration `0086` relabelled bronze and re-aggregated silver.
- **The dispatcher routes per (mailbox, prospect provider)** (`provider-placement.ts` pure, `provider-placement-read.ts`): provider from the MX (`recipient-mail-host.ts`, `recipient_mail_hosts`: `google` / `microsoft` = Microsoft 365 / `microsoft_consumer` = `*.olc.protection.outlook.com`; refreshed at the top of `runDispatch`, 30 d; failed lookup = `unknown`, retried 6 h; gateway/other = no rule). Evidence = newest tests pooled until 30 seeds (`poolPlacementTests`, under 16 d; one test never stands alone, PLACEMENT-POOL-30-1028). (1) a FIRST email needs Inbox there (>= 90 % of found); junk never; unmeasured only in COLD START (no production mailbox Inbox there yet); held = `heldPlacement`, rehomed to a mailbox Inbox there with room, else waits. (2) NO email, follow-ups included, from a mailbox MOSTLY in spam there (> 50 % of >= 10 found seeds, `mostlySpam`): `heldMostlySpam`, never moved (thread keeps its sender), waits until it measures better. `POST /orgs/send` does not filter (the dispatcher rehomes). Served on account-health as `googlePlacement` / `microsoftPlacement` / `microsoftConsumerPlacement`.
- **Stored daily series** `seed_placement_daily` (day x ESP x sending vendor), recomputed from bronze after every sync and at boot; `GET /internal/seed-placement/daily?days=` (admin chart: Google, Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com inbox %, apart; a row bronze no longer backs is deleted on refresh). **The fleet Google figure is dominated by the legacy Gandi senders** (since 2026-09-19 ~2/3 of seeds, 87-98 % spam) while Primeforge (~95 % of prospect sends) reads 0-13 % spam: always read it per vendor.
